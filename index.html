About FTF Canada

More than a game. A pathway forward.

First Touch Football Canada (FTF) is a football organisation headquartered in Toronto, Ontario, delivering and promoting opportunities for players across Canada.

FTF organises its own camps, combines and tournaments while using digital media and video coverage to help showcase player talent and achievements.

Need to know

Frequently asked questions

Where is First Touch Football Canada based?

FTF is headquartered in Toronto, Ontario, with events planned across Canada.

What age groups does FTF serve?

FTF programs include Next Gen camps for ages 6–13, high school opportunities for ages 14–18 and Pro Combines for aspiring players aged 18 and over. Individual event eligibility may vary.

Which formats of football does FTF cover?

FTF focuses on outdoor 11v11 football and indoor futsal.

How can I register for an event?

When an event is announced, its event page will provide the registration instructions, eligibility details, fees and registration link.

Does FTF provide player highlight videos?

FTF offers player promotion through digital media and video coverage. Contact the team for current service availability and booking details.
