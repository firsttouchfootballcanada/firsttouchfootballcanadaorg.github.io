Competitive placement

FTF uses its Competitive Index to guide team placement and support balanced matchups.

Team placement

FTF Competitive Index™

A team-placement guide intended to help create balanced matchups, stronger competition and improved experiences for players, coaches and clubs.

01

Tier 1 – Elite Level

Top-performing OPDL, APDL, BCSPL, PLSJQ, I-Model and leading district teams.

02

Tier 2 – High Performance

Highly competitive teams approaching elite status, including mid-table top-league and top-ranked district teams.

03

Tier 3 – Competitive

Solid district and I-Model teams, and developing teams in top-tier leagues.

04

Tier 4 – Development

Recreational and district-level teams focused on development and participation.

Placement note: Team tier selections are a placement tool and do not guarantee division placement. Final decisions remain at the discretion of the FTF Events Team.

Registration guidelines & important dates

Registration process

  1. Teams should submit full payment with their application. If full payment cannot be made on the application date, a booking deposit is required to secure the booking, with the remaining balance payable as agreed with the organizing committee.
  2. Teams are conditionally accepted, with details to follow.
  3. Additional team registration forms are sent after acceptance.
  4. Registration fees become non-refundable upon acceptance.
